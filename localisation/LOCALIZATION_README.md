Thank you for reading the Localization ReadMe!

This mod wouldn't be accessible to players around the world without your help, and we're firm believers in the idea that if you want to help, and you can help, then nothing should stop you.

All the localization in the folders marked 'replace' fulfill a very specific function in the Clausewitz Engine (Paradox games' in-house game engine). They REPLACE text that's already in the vanilla game. If you put original text in here, then it will still run. But if you're replacing anything that's in the vanilla files, it MUST go here, or it could cause problems, like crashing after the game loads to 100%.

In the future, we're going to work on removing old localization that isn't used anymore, and divide working replacement text into separate files so that it's easier for everyone to read, edit, and use. In the meantime, please put localization where it's supposed to go.

Thanks for playing our favorite game.
    -- the devs of EaC.
